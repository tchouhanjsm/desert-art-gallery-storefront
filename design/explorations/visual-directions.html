Typography proposal
Display: Comfortaa 700. Body: Lato 400/500. UI: Lato 600/700. Scale: 56/40/28/16/12 px desktop; 38/30/22/15/12 mobile. Sources: Google Fonts; SIL OFL 1.1 (Comfortaa has a reserved font name).
Layout & image art direction
Strong framed panels, alternating product/story bands, rounded or arched image windows and compact product cards. Real-life interior context paired with clean product views and graphic colour backgrounds.
Potential strengthsAccessible, energetic and memorable; product and editorial modules can alternate at a lively pace.
Trade-offs to testMay read less rarefied or premium if the colour balance becomes playful; requires careful restraint in labels and decorative shapes.
All examples are rendered design explorations using abstract panels, not authentic DAG product photographs. Font families are proposed open-font candidates and are used in this rendered study where locally available: Inter, Noto Sans Display, PT Mono, EB Garamond, Lato and Comfortaa. Google Fonts metadata records these families under the SIL Open Font License (OFL); PT Mono and Lato/Comfortaa contain reserved-name terms for modified versions. No font binaries are committed. Recheck exact family versions, weights, licence files and hosting before production. Colour pairs and font specifications are proposals for comparison only. Decorative accent colours must not be used as small text unless the foreground/background pair passes WCAG contrast. For text on accent fills, use the high-contrast ink specified in the direction specification. No customer-facing claim or product fact is asserted.
