## Closed scope

Do not add badges, testimonials, reviews, ratings, banners, newsletter, social feeds, animations, popups, sticky bars, chat, new icons, extra navigation, recommendations or other components/functions not listed above. Disabled/visual-only controls must stay disabled. No commerce, payment, cart, checkout, account, wishlist, live search/filter/sort, shipping calculation, returns, order or inventory workflow is included.
