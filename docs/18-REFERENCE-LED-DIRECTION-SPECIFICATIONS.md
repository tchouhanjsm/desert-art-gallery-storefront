# 18 — Reference-Led DAG Visual Territory Specifications

**Status:** Four new proposals for owner comparison. None is recommended, preferred, selected or approved.

**Owner decision basis:** D-008 rejected the earlier P-015 package. D-009 names POJ Studio and Macaroni Shoten as the principal references for the next design study. This is a fresh option set, not a relabelled version of the rejected one.

Rendered examples: [Reference-led desktop/mobile comparison board](../design/explorations/reference-led-directions.html). A separate M2-A review bundle contains 24 full-page renders: four unranked territories × homepage, shop/collection and product detail × 1440 px desktop and 390 px mobile. This is an owner-review artefact, not an approved page design.

## Design translation

The reference study supports a retail experience with:
- A clear campaign/brand first view and immediately understandable route into the catalogue.
- Compact, grouped product-category navigation and direct collection discovery.
- Product cards with visible names, price and truthful availability; sorting/filtering only where catalogue data supports them.
- A product detail experience with meaningful image sequence, variants, material/dimension/care facts and relevant maker/craft context.
- Distinct editorial and maker-story routes that enrich product discovery rather than replace it.
- Discoverable service, company and support information.
- Mobile layouts that preserve search, navigation, prices and core purchase actions.

POJ currently demonstrates a broader editorial artisan-commerce journey. Macaroni's live category structure is simpler, but its live product listings were empty during review, so current merchandising details could not be assessed. These proposals therefore take no assumptions from Macaroni's historic catalogue.

All options below are new DAG explorations. They do not copy reference-site logos, text, products, photos, proprietary assets or pixel-level composition. Every displayed object image in the board is a labelled abstract placeholder, not a real DAG product.

## Option 01 — Porcelain Index

**Concept:** A material-first gallery catalogue. Editorial typography, measured spacing and a clear product taxonomy give each object's form and material room to speak.

### Palette proposal

| Role | Name | HEX |
|---|---|---|
| Main background | Paper | `#F4F0E8` |
| Secondary surface | Surface | `#E3DED3` |
| Light surface | Porcelain | `#FBF9F4` |
| Primary text | Ink | `#242827` |
| Secondary text | Deep Moss | `#4C5550` |
| Accent 1 | Oxide | `#8A4F43` |
| Accent 2 | Spruce | `#3F5F53` |
| Border/rule | Stone Line | `#C8C2B7` |

### Typography

- Display/headings: Cormorant Garamond 600/700.
- Body: Manrope 400/500.
- UI/controls: Manrope 600/700.
- Suggested scale: desktop 64/44/30/16/12 px; mobile 42/34/25/15/12 px.
- Font source/licensing: Google Fonts families; SIL Open Font License 1.1. Review the exact release's copyright, OFL file and any Reserved Font Name terms before self-hosting or redistribution. Do not alter font binaries for this proposal.

### Layout and art direction

- 12-column responsive grid; generous but purposeful whitespace.
- Header: restrained wordmark and clear Shop, Collections, Stories, About, Search and Bag paths.
- Home: simple announcement strip, asymmetric text/image hero, four compact category doors, selected product grid and a material/craft editorial band.
- Shop: clean collection introduction, visible result count, factual sort/filter controls and 3-column desktop product grid.
- Product detail: large image gallery beside a compact facts/purchase column; maker/material/care details below.
- Editorial: split story panel with clear link back to related collection.
- Photography: high-detail daylight object studies, tactile close crops, quiet backdrop and restrained shadow. Let the product provide colour; avoid artificial colour treatment.
- Customer impression: measured, considered and gallery-led.

### Neutral trade-offs

Potential advantages: product details stay readable; easy to extend across different product types; material and provenance stories fit naturally.
Potential risks: may feel restrained if campaign imagery and collection curation are weak; image quality must be consistently strong; a very spacious grid can reduce visible product count on smaller screens.

### Contrast review

- Ink/Paper: 13.12:1.
- Deep Moss/Paper: 6.79:1.
- Oxide/Paper: 5.63:1.
- Spruce/Paper: 6.21:1.
All listed text pairings exceed WCAG AA 4.5:1 for normal text.

## Option 02 — Bordeaux Editions

**Concept:** A chapter-led collector's shop. Dark plum surfaces and expressive serif typography create a more atmospheric retail experience, while warm paper and gold details keep information structured.

### Palette proposal

|---|---|---|

| Secondary surface | Mulberry | `#493042` |
| Primary/light text | Porcelain | `#F6EBE9` |
| Secondary text | Rose Mist | `#DCCBD3` |
| Accent 1 | Rose | `#B85F73` |
| Accent 2 | Old Gold | `#D9B56D` |
| Border/rule | Mauve Line | `#80566D` |
| Deep text | Ink | `#22151D` |

### Typography

- Display/headings: Fraunces 500/600/700.
- Body: DM Sans 400/500.
- UI/controls: DM Sans 600/700.
- Suggested scale: desktop 66/46/30/16/12 px; mobile 42/34/24/15/12 px.
- Font source/licensing: Google Fonts families; SIL Open Font License 1.1. Review exact release copyright/OFL and any Reserved Font Name terms before self-hosting. Use original family names and unmodified font files.

### Layout and art direction

- 12-column grid with dark chapter surfaces and thin gold rules.
- Header: compact logo, Shop, Editions, Journal, Our Story, Search and Bag.
- Home: small campaign notice, large chapter-style hero, category doors, current edition product selection and a story chapter.
- Shop: collection presented as an edition with concise introduction, clear availability and visible price; product tiles stay on consistent image ratios.
- Product detail: paper-coloured facts panel against a dark editorial page; image gallery remains primary.
- Editorial: chapter numbers, maker/material context and related product routes.
- Photography: low-key studio still life with controlled warm light and tactile material detail. Use neutral white balance and preserve the true product colour.
- Customer impression: collected, intimate and story-rich.

### Neutral trade-offs

Potential advantages: strong atmosphere and a memorable storytelling frame; supports limited curated edits; clear separation between chapters and catalogue.
Potential risks: dark backgrounds can alter perceived product colour; long-form editorial may dominate shopping if overused; rose accent is not suitable as small text against plum.

### Contrast review

- Porcelain/Plum: 14.10:1.
- Rose Mist/Plum: 10.61:1.
- Old Gold/Plum: 8.46:1.
- Plum/Rose: 3.86:1 — use for large display or decorative accents only, not normal text.

## Option 03 — Blue Current

**Concept:** A contemporary studio catalogue with cool, clear surfaces, an open product grid and a controlled warm accent. Editorial context is present but catalogue discovery remains the primary journey.

### Palette proposal

| Role | Name | HEX |
|---|---|---|

| Secondary surface | Blue Slate | `#173B4A` |
| Primary/light text | Ice | `#E5F2F4` |
| Secondary text | Mint | `#B8D8C8` |
| Accent 1 | Mineral Cyan | `#4E9DA5` |
| Accent 2 | Apricot | `#F2A477` |
| Border/rule | Tide Line | `#56818A` |
| Light surface | Pale Mist | `#DDE9E6` |

### Typography

- Display/headings: Newsreader 500/600/700.
- Body: Work Sans 400/500.
- UI/controls: Work Sans 600/700.
- Suggested scale: desktop 62/44/29/16/12 px; mobile 40/33/24/15/12 px.
- Font source/licensing: Newsreader is released by Production Type under SIL Open Font License 1.1; Work Sans is a Google Fonts family under SIL Open Font License 1.1. Retain exact copyright/OFL notices with font distribution; verify the exact release before production. Do not modify font files.

### Layout and art direction

- 12-column grid; broad horizontal hero with a clear split between headline and image.
- Header: Shop All, Categories, Field Notes, About, Search and Bag.
- Home: campaign line, catalogue-led hero, four category doors, browseable product grid and field-note feature.
- Shop: collection introduction, result count, factual filters/sort and open 3- or 4-column grid.
- Product detail: gallery and facts side-by-side at desktop; stacked gallery and sticky/visible purchase summary at mobile.
- Editorial: short field notes about material, use and making, cross-linked to the relevant collection.
- Photography: natural studio daylight, honest colours, close material details and contextual scale shots where available.
- Customer impression: clear, contemporary and practical.

### Neutral trade-offs

Potential advantages: strong product readability and navigation; clear contrast; good fit for a catalogue with several product classes.
Potential risks: cool colour family may compete with warm textiles; dark surfaces require disciplined image calibration; an over-structured grid can feel less intimate.

### Contrast review

- Ice/Deep Navy: 12.48:1.
- Mint/Deep Navy: 9.32:1.
- Deep Navy/Mineral Cyan: 4.55:1.
- Deep Navy/Apricot: 7.05:1.
The Deep Navy/Mineral Cyan pair clears normal-text AA narrowly; validate actual rendered colour and font size in implementation.

## Owner-review board interpretation and quality checks

The companion HTML board is a comparative design study, not a live storefront, a production prototype or a pixel-accurate capture of either reference site. Its desktop and mobile labels identify the layout concept shown; the preview frames are sized for side-by-side comparison and do not claim fixed 1440 px or 390 px browser viewports.

The listed contrast ratios are calculated from the exact proposed HEX values using the WCAG 2.x relative-luminance formula and rounded to two decimal places. The repository test suite recalculates every documented foreground/background pairing. Any pairing below 4.5:1 must remain explicitly restricted from normal-sized text; the Bordeaux Plum/Rose pairing is documented as large-display or decorative use only.

The governance validator checks the board's English language and viewport metadata, single main landmark, skip navigation, page heading, labelled option articles, labelled desktop/mobile preview groups, unique IDs, image alternative text and visible keyboard focus styling. The test suite also recalculates the proposal contrast figures.

These automated checks do not replace browser-based responsive review, screen-reader testing, owner visual acceptance or the later M2 prototype review. They do not approve any palette, typography, photography direction or production visual system.
