# 02 — Brand Identity (Working Foundation)

## Brand
Desert Art Gallery (DAG).

## Brand foundation
A gallery and retail experience for textiles, objects and craft connected with Jaisalmer, Rajasthan. The brand should communicate discernment, material character, authenticity, considered curation and trust to a Japan-first customer audience.

## Desired customer impression
- Thoughtfully curated.
- Assured, not loud.
- Contemporary, not generic.
- Rooted in place without relying on clichés.
- Premium through quality of presentation and information, not inflated claims.
- Welcoming and easy to navigate.

## Brand voice
English-only, clear, warm, precise and restrained. Prefer concrete product facts over vague superlatives. Avoid claims such as "world's finest", "ancient tradition", "museum quality" or "sustainable" unless independently substantiated and approved.
