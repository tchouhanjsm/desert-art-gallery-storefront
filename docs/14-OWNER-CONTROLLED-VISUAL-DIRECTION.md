# Owner-Controlled Brand Identity

**Active rule: D-014 and `docs/DESIGN-AUTHORITY.md` govern.** The former visual-direction selection protocol in this file is superseded and preserved at `docs/historical/superseded/14-OWNER-CONTROLLED-VISUAL-DIRECTION.md` for audit only.

The v2 kit establishes structure, not brand identity. Final colours, typefaces/weights/hierarchy/type scale, logo/wordmark/descriptor and photography direction/crops remain exclusively owner-controlled and pending. Do not revive P-015 or P-016, infer values from the layout-only QA tokens, or select a brand direction on the owner's behalf.

Use `docs/OWNER-TOKEN-INPUT.md` to record exact owner-supplied values and `docs/DECISIONS.md` for approved decisions. No branded styling or production tokens may be finalized until approval is explicit and recorded.
