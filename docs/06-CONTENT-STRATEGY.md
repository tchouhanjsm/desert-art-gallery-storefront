# 06 — Content Strategy

## Language
All customer-facing content is English-only.

## Principles
- Accuracy before eloquence.
- Specific facts before generic claims.
- Clear product information before promotional storytelling.
- Explain material, dimensions, condition, maker and provenance only when verified.
- Distinguish known facts from unavailable information.
- Avoid invented heritage, maker biographies, techniques, origin stories, certifications and sustainability claims.

## Content types
- Product titles and descriptions.
- Materials and specifications.
- Dimensions and care information.
- Category descriptions.
- Maker profiles.
- Brand/about content.
- Journal/editorial.
- Customer guidance and FAQs.
- Shipping, returns, legal and contact information.

## Unknown information
Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction. Record missing information and use an approved neutral state or omit unsupported claims.

## Product description structure
Where supported:
1. Clear product identity.
2. Verified material and technique.
3. Dimensions and useful specifications.
4. Condition and known variations.
5. Care guidance.
6. Verified maker/provenance context.
7. Availability and purchasing information.

Do not force every product into a story template when facts are unavailable.
