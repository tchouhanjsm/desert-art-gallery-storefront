Four new visual territories developed after the owner rejected the first set. The study translates premium editorial retail, compact category discovery and clear product merchandising into a distinct Jaisalmer-rooted storefront. None is selected or recommended.

THE AI PROPOSES. THE OWNER CHOOSES. THE AI IMPLEMENTS.All four directions below are proposals only. Product shapes and catalogue names are abstract placeholders, not DAG products or owner photographs. Choose one, combine named elements, request revisions or reject all four. No production theme or final tokens are approved.
