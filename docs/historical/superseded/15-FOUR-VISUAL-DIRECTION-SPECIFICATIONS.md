## Direction 01 — Graphic Atelier

**Concept:** High-contrast modernist retail with art-book energy, assertive typography, numbered collection edits and hard-edged graphic composition.

### Exact palette proposal

| Role | Name | HEX |
|---|---|---|
| Main background | Paper | `#F8F1E7` |
| Secondary surface | Blush | `#F2C9B8` |
| Primary text | Cobalt | `#182C6B` |
| Secondary text | Slate Cobalt | `#43517C` |
| Accent 1 | Vermilion | `#F0442E` |
| Accent 2 | Marigold | `#E9B92F` |
| Light surface | Chalk | `#FFFDF8` |
| Border/rule | Cobalt | `#182C6B` |

### Typography
Display/heading: Inter 900, uppercase, tight tracking. Body: Inter 400/500. UI: Inter 600/700. Desktop scale 64/44/28/16/12 px; mobile 42/32/22/15/12 px. Sources: [Inter](https://github.com/rsms/inter), [Google Fonts metadata](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/inter/METADATA.pb). SIL OFL 1.1; verify exact release and OFL file before production.

### Layout and components
12-column grid, strong horizontal rules, asymmetric split hero, compact wordmark/navigation header, oversized headline, three-column product cards with visible labels, numbered collection edits, colour-band editorial modules.

### Photography direction
High-key direct product photography, controlled hard shadows, close material crops and graphic still lifes. Preserve real product colour.

### Neutral trade-offs
Potential strengths: immediate recognition, confident shelf impact, flexible for mixed categories. Risks: graphics may overpower intricate pieces; saturated surfaces can compete with product colour.

### Contrast notes
Cobalt/Paper 11.62:1; Cobalt/Blush 8.58:1; Slate Cobalt/Blush 5.11:1; #111111/Vermilion 5.01:1; Cobalt/Marigold 7.10:1. Use dark text on Vermilion and Marigold; not white.

## Direction 02 — Future Relics

**Concept:** Digital-future object gallery with deep ultramarine, electric accents, modular grids and technical product presentation.

### Exact palette proposal

| Role | Name | HEX |
|---|---|---|
| Main background | Night | `#0B103E` |
| Secondary surface | Ultramarine | `#1524A8` |
| Primary text | Ice | `#F6F7FF` |
| Secondary text | Signal Lavender | `#B9C5F7` |
| Accent 1 | Acid | `#D9FF3F` |
| Accent 2 | Laser Pink | `#F52C9A` |
| Border/rule | Ion | `#00B8D9` |
| Deep surface | Signal Navy | `#111956` |

### Typography
Display/heading: Noto Sans Display 800/700. Body/UI: PT Mono 400 (one regular weight; do not synthesize bold). Desktop scale 60/40/26/16/12 px; mobile 40/32/22/15/12 px. Sources: [Noto Sans](https://github.com/notofonts/latin-greek-cyrillic), [PT Mono details](https://fontsource.org/fonts/pt-mono/about). SIL OFL 1.1; PT Mono has Reserved Font Name terms.

### Layout and components
12-column modular grid, index-like header, split dark hero with electric abstract graphic, panel-based product detail, technical specification blocks, dark product tiles and structured collection codes.

### Photography direction
Controlled studio still lifes, reflective surfaces only where they suit the object, macro detail and precise shadows. No artificial glow on the product itself.

### Neutral trade-offs
Potential strengths: distinctive digital character and strong specification hierarchy. Risks: may feel technology-led rather than craft-led; dark/saturated surfaces can distort perceived product colour; mono copy can slow reading.

### Contrast notes
Ice/Night 16.98:1; Ice/Ultramarine 10.63:1; Signal Lavender/Ultramarine 6.70:1; Night/Acid 15.83:1; Night/Laser Pink 4.93:1; Ion/Night 7.65:1. Avoid Ice on Laser Pink for body text.

## Direction 03 — Living Garden

**Concept:** Expressive botanical and art-deco-inspired retail with layered greens, coral, citrus and violet.

### Exact palette proposal

| Role | Name | HEX |
|---|---|---|
| Main background | Canopy | `#063D35` |
| Secondary surface | Jade | `#0B766B` |
| Primary text | White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Secondary text | Mist | `#DAE8DD` |
| Accent 1 | Coral | `#FF6A3D` |
| Accent 2 | Citrus | `#F8F06A` |
| Border/decorative rule | Violet | `#6B4EFF` |
| Dark text on accents | Deep Ink | `#111111` |

### Typography
Display/heading: EB Garamond 700 Italic. Body: Lato 400/500. UI: Lato 600/700. Desktop scale 58/40/28/16/12 px; mobile 38/30/22/15/12 px. Sources: [EB Garamond metadata](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/ebgaramond/METADATA.pb), [Lato metadata](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/lato/METADATA.pb). SIL OFL 1.1; Lato has Reserved Font Name terms for modified derivatives.

### Layout and components
Editorial asymmetry with arches and framed imagery, expressive wordmark, short category navigation, serif headline, arched image windows with visible product facts and alternating editorial/product modules.

### Photography direction
Layered still life, tactile close-ups, natural light, controlled vibrant backdrops and human-scale context. Human/maker imagery must be real and authorized.

### Neutral trade-offs
Potential strengths: sensorial, warm and expressive; supports pattern and material variation. Risks: decoration may overwhelm comparison; storytelling could push product facts down.

### Contrast notes
White/Canopy 12.16:1; White/Jade 5.50:1; Mist/Canopy 9.60:1; Mist/Jade 4.34:1 (use white small text on Jade); Deep Ink/Coral 6.64:1; Deep Ink/Citrus 15.88:1. Violet/Canopy 2.41:1; decorative only, not for focus indicators.

## Direction 04 — Modern Folk Pop

**Concept:** Contemporary craft-pop retail pairing cobalt, saffron, pink and teal with rounded display lettering and energetic collection rhythm.

### Exact palette proposal

| Role | Name | HEX |
|---|---|---|
| Main background | Sunlight | `#FFF4D6` |
| Secondary surface | Pink | `#F05A8C` |
| Primary text | Cobalt | `#112C91` |
| Secondary text | Deep Slate | `#263763` |
| Accent 1 | Teal | `#13A69A` |
| Accent 2 | Saffron | `#F4B625` |
| Border/rule | Cobalt | `#112C91` |
| Dark text on Pink/Teal | Ink | `#111111` |

### Typography
Display/heading: Comfortaa 700. Body: Lato 400/500. UI: Lato 600/700. Desktop scale 56/40/28/16/12 px; mobile 38/30/22/15/12 px. Sources: [Comfortaa](https://github.com/googlefonts/comfortaa), [Lato metadata](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/lato/METADATA.pb). SIL OFL 1.1; both have Reserved Font Name terms.

### Layout and components
Framed panels, rounded/arched image windows, alternating product/story bands, bright asymmetric hero, expressive but legible wordmark, visible product facts and regular collection rhythm.

### Photography direction
Real-life interior/context images paired with clean product views, colour-grounded backgrounds and natural object scale. Do not force product colour to match accents.

### Neutral trade-offs
Potential strengths: energetic, approachable and memorable. Risks: may read less rarefied if every module is saturated; playful frames can compete with complex textile patterns.

### Contrast notes
Cobalt/Sunlight 10.69:1; Deep Slate/Sunlight 10.59:1; Cobalt/Pink 3.65:1 (large display only); Deep Ink/Pink 5.89:1; Deep Ink/Teal 6.25:1; Cobalt/Saffron 6.45:1. Avoid white on Teal/Saffron for small text.
