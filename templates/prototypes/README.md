# Prototype review templates

This folder contains owner decision and review records for future owner-authorized M2 validation. The current structural baseline is D-014's nine-page kit. Structural adoption does not authorize customer recruitment, usability sessions, branded visual evaluation or production work.

- `m2-owner-entry-decision.md` captures M2-A through M2-E. The former six-route shell scope is superseded; use the nine-page route list in `docs/PAGE-INVENTORY.md`.
- `prototype-review.csv` is a header-only evidence log.
- Use one CSV row per route/scenario/viewport review.
- Record viewport dimensions, date, outcome and screenshot/evidence reference.
- Keep participant identifiers, contact details, recordings, populated catalogue data and raw research notes out of this repository. Follow `docs/22-CUSTOMER-VALIDATION-PLAYBOOK.md`.
- Run `python3 scripts/audit_m2_readiness.py` to check that the five owner gates are present and consistently open.
- Do not refer to or reuse the historical P-015/P-016 visual-option boards; no DAG brand identity has been selected.
- `docs/23-M2-PROTOTYPE-ENTRY-AND-ACCEPTANCE.md` defines the D-014 nine-page structure and the remaining owner gates.
- The presence of these templates does not mean M2 testing, participant recruitment or brand approval has started.
