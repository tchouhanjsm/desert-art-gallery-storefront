# Private customer-research templates

These templates support the proposed workflow in [22 — Japan-First Customer Validation Playbook](../../docs/22-CUSTOMER-VALIDATION-PLAYBOOK.md).

## Use

1. Copy the blank templates into an owner-controlled private research workspace outside this Git repository.
2. Obtain owner approval for the cohort focus, recruitment route, moderator and any participant cost before recruiting.
3. Assign participant codes such as `R01`; do not put direct identifiers into the CSV files.
4. Obtain consent before note-taking. Ask separately for recording and identifiable-quote permission.
5. Keep recordings, contact details and any identifiable source material outside Git.
6. Link each finding to anonymized session/task references and retain contradictory evidence.

## Files

- `participant-screener.csv` — minimal eligibility outcome and participant code.
- `research-session-notes.csv` — de-identified interview context.
- `task-observations.csv` — task-level observations for prototype testing.
- `finding-synthesis.csv` — evidence-linked themes and implications.
- `session-opening-and-consent.md` — facilitator opening and consent prompts.
- `discovery-interview-guide.md` — Stage A discussion guide.
- `prototype-usability-guide.md` — Stage B task guide.

All CSV files are deliberately header-only. Do not add participant rows, personal data, recordings, transcripts or private contact details to the repository. These templates are research aids, not a privacy notice, legal consent form or proof of participant consent.
