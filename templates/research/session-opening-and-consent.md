# Session opening and consent prompts

Use this as a facilitator checklist. Adapt the wording to the session; do not present it as a legal consent form or privacy notice.

## Opening

“Thank you for taking part. We are studying how people discover and evaluate home, textile, craft and decorative products online. We are interested in your actual experiences and questions. We are not testing you, and there are no right or wrong answers.

Taking part is voluntary. You may skip any question or stop the session at any time. We will use an anonymized participant code in our working notes. Please avoid sharing payment details or other sensitive personal information.”

## Note-taking

“Is it okay if I take notes during the discussion? The notes will use a participant code rather than your name.”

Record the response and date in the facilitator's private study log, not in this repository.

## Optional recording

“Would you separately agree to audio recording to help us review what was said? Recording is optional. The session can continue without it.”

Ask separately before video recording. Do not record unless explicit permission is given. Do not treat permission to participate as permission to record or publish a quote.

## Optional quotation

“May we use a short, anonymized quotation in an internal research summary? You can say no and still take part.”

If permission is not given, paraphrase instead. Remove identifying details even when permission is given.

## Closing

“Is there anything important we did not ask? Is there anything you would like to correct or withdraw from your contribution?”

Thank the participant. Follow the owner-approved process for handling, access and deletion of any private research material.
