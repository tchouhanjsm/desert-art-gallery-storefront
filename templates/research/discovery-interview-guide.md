# Stage A — Discovery interview guide

**Proposed duration:** 30–45 minutes.  
**Prerequisite:** Owner confirms the cohort focus, recruitment route and moderator.  
**No prototype is required.**

## 1. Recent behaviour

- Tell me about the most recent time you searched for or bought a home, textile, craft or decorative object online.
- What started the search?
- Where did you look first? What did you do next?
- What alternatives did you compare, and why?
- What ultimately helped you decide?

## 2. Product evaluation

- What did you need to understand about the item before deciding?
- Which images or views were most useful?
- Were there facts you could not find or had trouble interpreting?
- How did you judge size, material, condition, quality or suitability?
- What information did you trust, and what did you verify elsewhere?

## 3. Seller and cross-border confidence

- What do you normally check about a seller before placing an order?
- Have you purchased from a seller outside Japan? Please describe the experience, if relevant.
- What questions did you have about delivery, duties/taxes, payment, returns or support?
- What would you need to know before considering a purchase from a seller in another country?

Do not suggest that DAG currently supports a particular carrier, payment method, destination, delivery time or returns policy.

## 4. Purchase or enquiry context

- Have you contacted a seller before purchasing an item? What prompted you?
- What information could you not resolve yourself?
- What made the response useful or unhelpful?
- In what circumstances would you expect to enquire rather than purchase directly?

Do not frame enquiry as the preferred or approved DAG model.

## 5. Language and comprehension

- When reviewing an English-language product page, which wording or information is clear?
- Are there any terms, units or descriptions that you would need explained?
- Which parts would you verify before proceeding?

Do not translate the interface during an English-only comprehension task. Record observable comprehension issues without inferring a participant's general language ability.

## 6. Close

- What have I missed?
- What would you want a gallery like DAG to explain more clearly?
- Is there anything you want corrected or excluded from the notes?

## Facilitator discipline

Ask open questions and follow actual examples. Do not show historical P-015/P-016 proposals or ask participants to vote on brand identity. D-014 and the active page/component inventories define the current structural scope. Record observations and participant reports separately. Use the session ID and participant code only.
